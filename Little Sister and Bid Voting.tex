	\maketitle
	
	The prudential board has found it necessary to allow for the voting requirements to flex with the number of active chapter members. The changes are as follows:
	
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item If the chapter is at or below 18 members, to appoint a new sister or bid a new member, a vote of yes from $\frac{3}{4}$ths of the \textbf{present, active chapter} is required.	
		\item If the chapter is above 18 members, to appoint a new sister or bid a new member, a vote of yes from $\frac{3}{4}$ths of the \textbf{total active chapter} is required.
	\end{enumerate}
	
	This bylaw was ratified on 9/24/2026 with a vote of 3-1-0.
