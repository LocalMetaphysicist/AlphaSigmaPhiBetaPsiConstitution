A brother of Alpha Sigma Phi has the right to request financial assistance with paying for dues/housing if doing so in a typical fashion would cause him to undergo financial strain. Financial need will be determined by the prudential board, as this plan should be used \underline{only} under the circumstances that paying in a normal fashion would cause financial distress. Alpha Sigma Phi offers two options for financial deference.\\

\underline{Monthly Payment Plan}

A brother may request a monthly payment plan for dues or rent. Each month, for four months, he will be charged one fourth of the dues amount and/or one fourth of the housing amount. This amount will be charged without interest. Late payments will result in a fine of \$50 one-time and \$5 for each late day. The once a month charge will be due on the 20th of each month.\\

\underline{One Year Deference}

A brother has the right to request a deferred payment of up to one year, with an interest charge of 8\% on the total amount. No monthly charge will be incurred, only the 8\% surcharge on the total amount. This payment will be due exactly one year from the date of agreement. Failure to pay on time will result in a fine of \$200 one-time and \$5 for each late day.

The details of both plans are open to discussion between the prudential board and the party(ies) involved in making the agreement, and prudential board reserves the right to allow for extensions on either plan if deemed necessary.

Prudential board \textbf{DOES NOT} have the right to modify any agreement made without \textbf{EXPRESS CONSENT} of the involved party(ies). Any agreement made will involve a signed contract between Alpha Sigma Phi Beta Psi and the individual(s) requesting financial assistance.
