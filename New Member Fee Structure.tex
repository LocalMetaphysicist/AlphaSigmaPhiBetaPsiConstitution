	The changes made to the new member fee structure from nationals as of 9/24/2026 is as follows:
	
	\begin{itemize}
		\item New members will be charged new member dues directly through bill highway. They have two options for payment:
		\begin{itemize}
			\item \$200 at signing the Membership Agreement Form, and \$825 due by the completion of scenes 3 and 4 of the initiation ceremony. This totals to \$1025
			\item \$210 every month for two months, and then \$84 every month for 10 months. This totals to \$1050.
		\end{itemize}
	\end{itemize}
	
	As a result, the Prudential board has adopted the following plan to assist new members with covering these fees:
	
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Upon completion of the New Member Orientation process, the chapter will reimburse the new members for \$500 of the new member fee. The chapter will then charge the new members an additional \$250 on top of regular dues the next two semesters to cover this cost.
	\end{itemize}
	
	Prude ratified this bylaw on 9/24/2026 with a vote of 4-0-0
