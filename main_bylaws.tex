\section*{Bylaws}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Unknown Date} - Standards Board \& Standards Board Constitution
    \item \textbf{3/8/2018} - The right to invite guests to the house supersedes the right to deny them access.
    \item \textbf{2/10/2019} - A Sober Host program has been created.
    \item \textbf{4/26/2019} - Yearlong positions will henceforth begin at the end of the spring semester they are elected and Terminate at the end of the spring semester of the following year.
    \item \textbf{3/5/2020} - \$100 is to be collected from each brother at the beginning of each semester for the purposes of fines.
    \item \textbf{10/23/2022} - Committee Position Requirements
    \item \textbf{04/09/23} - Excuses for rituals are granted either by HM or a majority of Prudential Board.
    \item \textbf{04/25/24} - Brothers who are initiated on or after the 26th of August 2024 are required to hold active membership of an RPI recognized club.
    \item \textbf{11/19/2024} - Fines for missing chapter shall be applied as follows: A \$5 fine for missing chapter, and a \$5 fine for missing details. Attendance will be recorded separately.
    \item \textbf{11/19/2024} - Financial Assistance Plan
\end{enumerate}
